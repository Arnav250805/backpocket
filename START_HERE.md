## 🚀 First Steps

### 1. Open the App
Navigate to: **http://localhost:5173**

### 2. Add Your First Entry
1. Click the blue **+** button (bottom-right)
2. Fill in a name and date
3. Try the voice-to-text feature!
4. Click **Save Entry**

### 3. Try Searching
Type in the search bar to find your entry

### 4. Export Your Data
Click the **Export** button to download a backup

---

## 🎤 Cool Features to Try

### Voice-to-Text
1. Click **Start Voice Input** in the notes field
2. Speak into your microphone
3. Watch the text appear automatically!

### Audio Recording
1. Click **Record Audio**
2. Say something
3. Click **Stop Recording**
4. Play it back!

### Fuzzy Search
1. Add a few entries
2. Try searching with typos (e.g., "Jon" finds "Johnson")
3. Search by topic (e.g., "Work")
